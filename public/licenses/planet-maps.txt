Planet colour maps used by the three-dimensional map (public/bodies/maps/*.jpg),
each resampled to 1024x512 by scripts/fetch-planet-maps.ts. All NASA/USGS
material, in the public domain.

mercury  MESSENGER MDIS global mosaic (2013). NASA/JHUAPL/Carnegie Institution of Washington.
venus    Magellan radar cylindrical map. NASA/JPL/USGS.
earth    Blue Marble, land_shallow_topo_2048. NASA Visible Earth (Reto Stöckli, NASA Earth Observatory).
moon     LRO LROC WAC colour mosaic, CGI Moon Kit. NASA/GSFC Scientific Visualization Studio. https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/4720
mars     Viking MDIM 2.1 colorized mosaic. NASA/JPL/USGS.
jupiter  Cassini ISS cylindrical map, PIA07782 (December 2000). NASA/JPL/Space Science Institute.

Saturn, Uranus and Neptune have no public-domain NASA colour map we could
find and are drawn as plain tinted spheres; Saturn's rings are an untextured
disc. Every body leans on its IAU north pole; Sun and planets keep their true
proportions to one another but are not to scale with the compressed distances. Sublight is an independent project, not affiliated
with or endorsed by NASA or any space agency.
