Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 August 2026

Sublight is an independent project: a map of the solar system's active robotic spacecraft, drawn by the travel time of their light. It is available as a website (sublight.observer) and as an iOS app. This policy explains what happens to your information. The short version: Sublight does not collect it.

What we collect

Nothing. Sublight has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no trackers, and no third-party SDKs that profile you. We do not collect, store, sell, or share any personal data. We do not know who you are.

Network requests

The app and website fetch public data and imagery directly from NASA and related sources (JPL Horizons, mars.nasa.gov, DSCOVR/EPIC, the NASA Image Library) and are hosted on Cloudflare. As with any internet request, those services receive standard technical information such as your IP address in order to deliver the response. That data is handled under their own privacy policies, not ours, and we never receive it.

Notifications (iOS)

If you turn on "New imagery alerts", the app occasionally checks a public data file to see whether a spacecraft has sent home a newer frame, and if so shows you a local notification on your device. This check happens on your device. No notification token, device identifier, or personal data is sent to us or to any server we control.

Purchases (iOS)

Sublight is free. The optional "Support development" tips are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. Apple processes the payment; we never see or store your name, card, or payment details. Apple's privacy policy applies to that transaction.

Children

Sublight is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Third parties

  • NASA / JPL and related services provide the public data and imagery.
  • Apple processes optional in-app tips and app distribution.
  • Cloudflare hosts the website and app data files.

Sublight is not affiliated with or endorsed by NASA or any space agency.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above will be updated. Material changes will be reflected on this page.

Contact

Questions? Reach out at contact@sublight.observer.
